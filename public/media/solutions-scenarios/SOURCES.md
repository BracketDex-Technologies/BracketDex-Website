# Change Scenarios illustration sources

Four independent transparent PNG illustrations were generated on October 30, 2026, from the selected fresh Solutions direction 2, `fresh-directions/solutions-2.png`, in the local design evidence folder. They are not crops of the page mockup.

- `before.png` (1491 × 1055): scattered paperwork and requests with wandering routes.
- `change.png` (2172 × 724): document, owner and decision sequence. Stage labels are editable HTML.
- `after.png` (1469 × 1071): visible work queue, owner and clear next steps.
- `notebook.png` (1586 × 992): notebook, checklist, pen, orange note and coffee cup.

These drawings are illustrative concepts. The alternate scenarios reuse the same conceptual before/decision/after visual vocabulary with their own operational copy. Brand artwork, protected problem cards, Navbar and Footer come from the existing site.
